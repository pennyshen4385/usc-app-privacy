University Senior College app – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The University Senior College app helps students and visitors of University Senior College (USC) to find buildings, rooms and offices on the campus map. It is provided by University Senior College at Adelaide University Incorporated (ABN 79 837 939 148), Adelaide, South Australia ("USC", "we").

The short version

The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, student number or any other personal details.
  • It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • It does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not share anything with advertisers or other third parties.

What stays on your device

If you use the "My places" star or look at places, the app remembers which places you saved and the last few places you viewed, so you can find them again quickly. This list is stored only on your own phone. It is never sent to USC or anyone else. You can remove it at any time by un-starring a place, or by deleting the app, which deletes this data.

The map information

The campus map, the list of buildings and places and their directions are part of the app. They are the same for every user. The app works without an internet connection.

Children and students

The app is intended for the students, staff and visitors of USC, who are in Years 10 to 12 or adults. Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing about a student that we collect or keep.

Changes to this policy

If a later version of the app collects anything, for example to keep the map up to date, we will update this policy before that version is released and say so in the app's release notes.
